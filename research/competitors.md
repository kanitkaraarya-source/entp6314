# Competitor Scan: Top 5 Student Organization / Club Management Products

Research date: 2026-09-16.

**Methodology & limitation, carried over from prior research files in this folder:** direct page fetches (WebFetch) to G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit, Apple App Store, and Google Play are blocked by network egress policy in this environment. All pricing figures, feature descriptions, and review quotes below were obtained via the web search layer's snippets of the cited pages — attributed to a specific URL, but **not independently re-fetched and character-verified against the live page**. Where a fact could not be pinned to one authoritative source, or where sources conflicted, it is marked **[UNVERIFIED]**.

Selection note: I picked 5 distinct, currently-marketed products that show up repeatedly as the major players serving student-organization/club management — three higher-ed-focused incumbents (Anthology Engage, CampusGroups, Modern Campus Involve/Presence) plus two broader club/association platforms that student orgs, alumni chapters, and Greek chapters commonly use in practice (WildApricot, TidyHQ). GroupMe/Discord/Google Sheets are excluded — they're general-purpose tools, not club-management products, and are covered in `opportunity-scan.md` instead.

## What does nobody serve well?

Putting the five side by side, three gaps show up in every single row and none of the five close:

1. **Nobody gives a volunteer-run club a dedicated, compliant way to handle money.** All five are org/event/membership tools first and payment-compliance tools never. WildApricot's own reviewers flag its payment processing as "problematic"; TidyHQ only adds payments at all on its $69/mo Pro tier; none of the five products (including the three enterprise higher-ed platforms) market a feature that solves the specific, dated problem documented in `complaints.md` — a club treasurer collecting dues through a personal Venmo and now facing 1099-K exposure under the 2025/2026 IRS threshold drop. This is a real, current, and unaddressed gap: the enterprise platforms are too expensive and too institution-gated for a $2k-budget club to ever touch, and the two "accessible" platforms (WildApricot, TidyHQ) treat payments as a bolt-on billing feature, not a compliance/liability solution. **[This synthesis point is my own inference from the table above, not a claim any source makes directly.]**

2. **Nobody serves the individual club well when the institution has already bought a platform for it.** The three enterprise products (Anthology Engage, CampusGroups, Modern Campus Involve) are sold and priced at the institution level — a single club officer has zero purchasing power and gets whatever configuration their student-affairs office chose, which is exactly why the complaint pattern across all three is the same: clunky, slow, hard for a new (i.e., every year, since officers turn over annually) user to learn. The institution is the actual customer; the club officer is a captive end user whose feedback has no pricing leverage. This structural mismatch — enterprise sales motion serving a first-time, one-year-tenure end user — shows up as near-identical UX complaints across three unrelated codebases and three different PE owners, which suggests it's a go-to-market problem, not a product-quality problem any one vendor is likely to out-execute.

3. **Nobody serves the "too small to be an institution, too complex to be a spreadsheet" middle well.** TidyHQ comes closest with a genuine free tier, but its own complaints (no batch import, broken membership-level reporting, no mobile app) show it's built for the smallest, simplest clubs and starts to strain once a club has real operational complexity (elections, budgets that need approval workflows, multi-tier membership). WildApricot is priced and positioned similarly but for general associations, not specifically for the annual-officer-turnover, low-budget, student-run pattern. None of the five is built around the defining structural fact of a student club: **the entire leadership team changes every year**, which is precisely the failure mode showing up in `opportunity-scan.md` and `complaints.md` (lost records, no institutional memory, starting over each fall) — and it's a gap that's orthogonal to, and unaddressed by, all three enterprise platforms' event/attendance-tracking feature depth.

**Net:** the competitive set splits cleanly into "enterprise platform the club didn't choose and doesn't control" (Anthology Engage, CampusGroups, Modern Campus Involve) versus "generic small-org software not built for the annual-turnover, low-budget, compliance-exposed reality of a student club specifically" (WildApricot, TidyHQ). No product in the top 5 is purpose-built around student-officer turnover and treasurer compliance risk at once — that combination is the whitespace.
